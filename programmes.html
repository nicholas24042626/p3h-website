Practical support. Everyday well-being.

Humanistic Hands and Heart (P3H) combines community care and digital tools to strengthen caregiver resilience and offer meaningful respite.

A community initiative by Tzu Chi Singapore

Soft-launched on 1 April 2026

GLOW (Nanyang), a Tzu Chi Active Ageing Centre, developed P3H in collaboration with digital enablement and community technology partner Healthy-Tec.

Digital activities available around the clock

Small steps for your well-being

Access the P3H platform on tablets at GLOW or install it on your personal phone or tablet. Ask the team for help getting started.

The platform brings together activities designed by clinical researchers and wellness professionals across five categories of support.

Cognitive

Make room for reflection

Mindfulness meditation, lessons about burnout and grief, guided SMART goals and a personalised sleep plan.

Physical

Care for your body

Stretching, chair yoga, a visual breathing guide and simple nutrition ideas.

Emotional

Check in with yourself

A daily mood log to record feelings and track patterns, plus guided journal prompts for private reflection.

Practical

Find a moment for you

A time audit tool to make space for personal time and a daily symptom log with self-care reminders.

Social

Stay connected

Peer support groups, buddy check-ins and community questions and answers with experts and peers.

24/7 access refers to digital wellness activities. Staff contact hours remain Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm.
