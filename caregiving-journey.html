One step at a time一步一步来

I may become a caregiver我可能快要成为看护者了

You do not need to have everything worked out. Begin by understanding what help your loved one may need and who can share the responsibilities.

A place to begin

  • Ask the care team what support may be needed at home and which skills you should learn.
  • Talk with your loved one about their preferences and who they want involved.
  • Discuss with family or trusted people who can help with appointments, meals, transport and time off.
  • Write down your biggest question about care, work, study or costs, and bring it to your first conversation.

AIC: understanding your caregiving learning needs offers further guidance. The external resource is in English.

您不需要一下子把所有事情安排好。可以先了解亲友可能需要什么帮助，以及有谁能分担看护责任。

可以从这里开始

  • 向医疗团队了解亲友在家中需要哪些照顾，以及您需要学习哪些技巧。
  • 与需要照顾的亲友聊聊，了解他们的意愿，以及希望哪些人参与。
  • 与家人或信任的人商量，谁能帮忙安排预约、准备餐食、接送，或在您休息时接手照顾。
  • 记下目前最困扰您的一个问题，无论是看护、工作、学业还是费用，都可以在第一次咨询时提出。

I am caring for someone now我正在照顾亲友

Choose the pressure that feels most urgent today. You can ask about more than one kind of support.

  • Money and applications: explore grants and help with navigating support.
  • Daily care: explore home support, meals and transport information.
  • Time to rest: ask about respite arrangements and current availability.
  • Someone to talk to: explore peer support and community connections.

Sharing responsibility can start with one specific request: an errand, a meal, or someone accompanying your loved one to an appointment.

先从今天最迫切的需要开始。您可以同时咨询多种支持。

  • 费用与申请：了解补助及申请协助。
  • 日常照护：了解居家服务、餐食和交通安排。
  • 休息时间：咨询短期暂托服务，让自己有时间休息。
  • 找人聊聊：了解与其他看护者交流的机会及社区资源。

可以先请别人帮一件具体的小事，例如跑腿、准备一餐，或陪亲友看医生。

Your needs still matter您的需要依然重要

Life after caregiving照顾亲友的责任改变后

Your loved one may have recovered, moved into residential care, begun receiving support from someone else, or died. Your daily responsibilities may have changed, even while your connection and concern continue.

If you are grieving

Grief has no fixed timetable. AIC's guidance describes feelings such as sadness, guilt, relief and numbness. Its Managing Emotions resource includes information about grief and bereavement support. This external resource is in English.

Finding your next rhythm

  • Consider one manageable routine: a regular meal, a short outing or a conversation with someone you trust.
  • If work or study is on your mind, talk through a gradual return with your employer, school or an appropriate adviser.
  • If care has moved to a centre or another person, ask how you can stay involved in a way that works for you and your loved one.

Connection without pressure

Ask P3H whether any current activities or community connections are suitable for your changed situation. Availability and participation arrangements need confirmation. You do not need to volunteer or mentor others to deserve support.

您的亲友可能已经康复、入住护理机构、改由他人照顾，或已离世。虽然牵挂仍在，您的日常责任和生活节奏可能已有很大的改变。

如果您正在经历哀伤

面对失落，每个人需要的时间都不同。护联中心的指南提到，悲伤、内疚、松一口气，甚至一时没有感觉，都可能是哀伤的一部分。您可以阅读护联中心的情绪管理指南（英文），了解面对哀伤的方法及丧亲支持资源。

慢慢找回生活节奏

  • 先做一件自己应付得来的小事，例如按时吃饭、出门走走，或找信任的人聊聊。
  • 如果您正在考虑回去工作或继续学业，可以与雇主、学校或相关顾问讨论如何慢慢适应。
  • 如果已由机构或其他人接手照顾，可以商量如何以适合您和亲友的方式继续参与。

按自己的步调保持联系

您可以向 P3H 询问，目前有哪些活动或社区资源适合您的情况。能否参加及具体安排，须向团队确认。即使您暂时没有精力当义工或帮助别人，也一样值得获得关怀。

Space for your life, too也为自己的生活留出空间

Young caregivers青年看护者

Do you help someone at home with meals, appointments, communication or everyday tasks? You may be balancing caring with school, work, friendships and plans for your future. You can ask for help even if you do not call yourself a caregiver.

You do not have to carry this alone

  • School and deadlines: speak to a teacher, school counsellor or student-support adviser about how caring affects attendance or assignments. Ask what support may be available.
  • Someone you trust: choose an adult you feel safe talking to. You can begin with “Things at home are affecting my studies and I need help.”
  • Time for yourself: talk with a trusted adult about sharing responsibilities so there is room for rest, friends and activities you enjoy.
  • Tasks that feel unsafe: ask a trusted adult or the care team for help with tasks you have not been trained or feel able to do.

Before you share personal information

You can begin with a general question. Before giving names or health details, ask the P3H team who will see your information, how it is used, and whether parent or guardian involvement is needed. Ask them to explain any limits to confidentiality, including safety concerns.

Age requirements, consent arrangements and suitable activities need to be confirmed with the team. The website does not provide a confidential counselling chat.

您是否会帮家人准备餐食、安排看医生、协助沟通或处理日常事务？除了这些责任，您可能还要兼顾学业、工作、朋友和未来的打算。即使您从没想过自己也是“看护者”，也可以寻求帮助。

您不必独自承担

  • 上课与交功课：如果照顾家人影响了上课或交功课，可以向老师、学校辅导员或学生事务人员说明情况，问问能获得什么帮助。
  • 信任的人：找一位让您觉得安心、愿意听您说话的成年人。可以这样开口：“家里的事情影响了我的学业，我需要帮助。”
  • 自己的时间：与信任的成年人商量如何分担责任，让自己有时间休息、见朋友，做喜欢的事。
  • 让您觉得不安全的看护工作：如果某些看护工作您没有学过，或觉得自己做不来，请向信任的成年人或医疗团队求助。

分享个人资料之前

您可以先提出一般性问题。在提供姓名或健康资料前，先问问 P3H 团队：谁会看到这些资料、资料会怎么使用，以及是否需要家长或监护人参与。也可以请团队说明，哪些情况下无法完全保密，例如涉及安全问题时会怎样处理。

年龄要求、同意程序和适合参加的活动，都需要向团队确认。本网站没有提供保密的网上心理辅导聊天服务。
