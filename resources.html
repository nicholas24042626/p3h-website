Frequently asked questions

Can I ask for support if my caregiving role has ended or changed?

You are welcome to ask the team about suitable resources and referral options. Participation in P3H activities depends on current arrangements. Our life after caregiving guide offers a starting point for changed routines, connection and bereavement resources.

I am a young caregiver. Where can I start?

Our young caregiver guide covers school, trusted adults and asking for help. Ask the team about age requirements, consent and privacy before sharing personal details.

Can I switch languages or use larger text?

Use the English / 中文 buttons at the top of any page to change the website language. English is the default on your first visit. Your choice is kept as you browse. Use Larger to enlarge the text, or Print this page to print or save a PDF. External websites and documents keep their original language.

What is P3H?

P3H stands for Humanistic Hands and Heart, a Tzu Chi Singapore programme that aims to reduce caregiver burden, strengthen resilience and provide timely respite.

Who is P3H for?

P3H supports youth and adult caregivers, especially those whose needs are not adequately met by existing subsidised services or private care options. Contact the team to discuss participation requirements.

What support can I explore?

The four support areas are care navigation and financial assistance; caregiver training and planning ahead; self-care and respite support; and peer support and resource sharing. Explore the support areas.

Where and when do activities take place?

Contact the P3H team for current schedules, venue details and access arrangements before attending an activity.

How do I get started?

Visit Contact to register for caregiver support or message a GLOW team. Tell us what support you are looking for.

Is the team available 24/7?

The P3H team’s contact hours are Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm.

Are grants and subsidies guaranteed?

Care navigation includes help with grant and subsidy applications. Each scheme has its own eligibility requirements and approval process. Ask the team about suitable schemes and any costs associated with programme participation.
