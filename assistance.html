Other assistance schemes

We help caregivers with referrals to suitable services. The colour markers show the type of support each scheme provides. Contact our GLOW teams to discuss your needs.

Care at home and getting around

HPC+

Enhanced Home Personal Care

Home-based personal care and support for people who need help with daily living activities.

View service details at AIC →

MET

Medical Escort and Transport

Transport and/or an escort for people who cannot travel independently to medical appointments.

Ask about a referral →

EASE

Enhancement for Active Seniors

Subsidies for eligible Singapore Citizen households to install senior-friendly fittings at home.

View EASE details at HDB →

SMF

Seniors' Mobility and Enabling Fund

Subsidies for eligible seniors who need mobility aids, assistive devices or home healthcare items.

Explore equipment grants →

Food and work opportunities

MOW

Meals on Wheels

Meal deliveries for homebound people who cannot buy or prepare meals and do not have a caregiver who can help.

View meal delivery details at AIC →

THKMC partnership

Micro-jobs

Simple micro-job opportunities matched to caregivers' skills and financial needs, in partnership with Thye Hua Kwan Moral Charities (THKMC).

Ask about micro-jobs →

Food From The Heart · FFTH

Community Food Pack

A monthly food pack containing 10–12 items, such as rice, cooking oil and instant noodles, delivered to our centres.

Ask about food packs →

Monitoring support

Limited quantity

BOP Monitor and BOP Sensor

Tracks activity patterns and notifies caregivers about unusual changes, such as altered sleep patterns or longer-than-usual bathroom visits.

Nanyang · Limited quantity

Caregiver 24.7
