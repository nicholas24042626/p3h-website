How we help

Financial grants

P3H helps caregivers explore financial support schemes and navigate grant applications.

Daily living costs

South West District

South West Community Caring Fund

S$250 in immediate support for eligible lower-income senior households and families with young children in the South West District.

View fund details →

ComCare · SMTA

Short-to-Medium-Term Assistance

Temporary financial support for lower-income individuals and families who are temporarily unable to work, looking for work or earning a low income.

View ComCare details →

ComCare · LTA

Long-Term Assistance

Support for people permanently unable to work due to old age, illness or disability, with little or no financial means or family support.

View ComCare details →

Caregiving support

South West District

South West Caregiver Support Fund

Financial assistance for eligible caregivers in the South West District to help with self-care and caregiving expenses.

View fund details (PDF) →

HCG

Home Caregiving Grant

Monthly cash payouts of up to S$600 to help eligible families care for persons with moderate to severe disabilities.

View HCG details at AIC →

Training and equipment

CTG

Caregivers Training Grant

Subsidies for approved caregiver training courses, helping caregivers learn practical skills at a lower out-of-pocket cost.

View CTG details at AIC →

SMF

Seniors' Mobility and Enabling Fund

Subsidies for eligible seniors who need assistive devices, such as wheelchairs or hospital beds, and home healthcare consumables.

Explore SMF support at AIC →

Eligibility and approval depend on each scheme.
