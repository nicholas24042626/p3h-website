Caregiver Training & Planning Ahead

Build practical caregiving skills and prepare for future care needs.

Build practical skills

P3H includes caregiver training alongside professional counselling and other holistic support services. Training helps you develop skills and confidence for your caring responsibilities.

Prepare for changing needs

Forward care planning helps you think ahead about future care needs and explore relevant support with the team.

Is this support for me?

P3H is designed for youth and adult caregivers, particularly those whose needs are not adequately met by existing subsidised services or private care options.

Speak with our team about suitable support, participation requirements and any costs before making arrangements.
