Self-Care & Respite Support

Make time to recharge through wellness activities and short-term care support.

Make space for your well-being

Wellness and self-care activities offer opportunities to rest, reconnect and build resilience.

Explore short-term care support

The April 2026 release describes short-term respite care at home or at the centre, helping caregivers attend urgent appointments and bridge waits for institutional care. Digital wellness activities include stretching, chair yoga, breathing guidance and mood check-ins. Contact the team to discuss your needs and current availability.

Is this support for me?

P3H is designed for youth and adult caregivers, particularly those whose needs are not adequately met by existing subsidised services or private care options.

Speak with our team about suitable support, participation requirements and any costs before making arrangements.
