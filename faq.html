Your questions, answered

A little clarity for your next step

Learn about P3H, the support areas and how to contact our team.

What is P3H?

P3H stands for Humanistic Hands and Heart, a Tzu Chi Singapore programme that aims to reduce caregiver burden, strengthen resilience and provide timely respite.

Who is P3H for?

P3H supports youth and adult caregivers, especially those whose needs are not adequately met by existing subsidised services or private care options. Contact the team to discuss participation requirements.

What support can I explore?

The four support areas are care navigation and financial assistance; caregiver training and planning ahead; self-care and respite support; and peer support and resource sharing. Explore the support areas.

Where and when do activities take place?

Contact the P3H team for current schedules, venue details and access arrangements before attending an activity.

How do I get started?

Complete the caregiver support registration form (opens in a new tab), or message 6859 1962 on WhatsApp or email caregiversupport@tzuchi.org.sg. Tell us what support you are looking for.

Is the team available 24/7?

The P3H team’s contact hours are Monday to Friday, 9am–5pm. The P3H platform offers 24/7 digital wellness activities on a personal phone or tablet. Centre tablet access is subject to GLOW opening arrangements. Digital access does not mean round-the-clock staff or respite care.

Are grants and subsidies guaranteed?

Care navigation includes help with grant and subsidy applications. Each scheme has its own eligibility requirements and approval process. Ask the team about suitable schemes and any costs associated with programme participation.

How can I access digital wellness activities?

The platform can be accessed on tablets at GLOW or installed on a personal phone or tablet. Ask the P3H team for installation and access guidance.
