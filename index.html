Who we help

Bridging the gaps in caregiver support

Existing subsidised services may not meet every caregiver’s needs, while private care can be difficult to afford. P3H supports youth and adult caregivers in this “missing middle”.

Find out who P3H supports
2,800caregivers targeted over three years

Our ambition: ease caregiving pressures, build resilience and create opportunities for timely respite.

Our Partners

Programme provider

Tzu Chi Singapore

The foundation brings community care and caregiver support together through Humanistic Hands and Heart.

Visit Tzu Chi Singapore →

Community care setting

GLOW (Nanyang)

A Tzu Chi Active Ageing Centre where P3H was soft-launched on 1 April 2026. Caregivers can use platform tablets at GLOW.

About visiting GLOW →

Technology collaboration

Healthy-Tec

The digital enablement and community technology partner that collaborated with GLOW to develop P3H's physical and digital support approach.

Caregiver training & peer support partner

Mindfull Community

Mindfull Community partners with P3H to provide C2C training and C4C peer support for caregivers.

Activity volunteer partner

Republic Polytechnic

Volunteers from Republic Polytechnic help with P3H activities such as scrapbooking and bookmark making.

Grant support

Income OrangeAid and NCSS

P3H is supported by the Income OrangeAid Caregiver Support Accelerator Grant, a strategic partnership between Income Insurance and the National Council of Social Service. Tzu Chi is one of 10 social service agencies in the first cohort.

Read the official grant announcement →

Eldercare training partner

The Salvation Army — Jade Circle Acaredemy

The Salvation Army's Jade Circle Acaredemy offers eldercare courses for care professionals, caregivers and the community, including dementia and palliative care, with SkillsFuture course fee subsidies of up to 70%.

Visit Jade Circle Acaredemy →
