Plan your visit

In-person support

Start at GLOW

P3H was soft-launched at GLOW (Nanyang), a Tzu Chi Active Ageing Centre, on 1 April 2026.

The programme includes short-term respite care at home or at the centre. Speak with the team about suitable care arrangements before visiting.

Plan your visit

Ask before travelling

Contact the P3H team to confirm the centre address, activity times, accessibility and any appointment requirements.

Not sure how to begin?

Our caregiving journey guide has conversation starters for future, current, former and young caregivers. You can begin with a general question. Before sharing health details, ask the team how your information will be used and who can see it.

Young caregivers can ask whether a parent or guardian needs to be involved and what limits to confidentiality apply.
